What is an Ankana and how much is it in Sq Ft? +
An Ankana (అంకణం) is a traditional land measurement unit used in Andhra Pradesh. 1 Ankana = 72 Sq Ft. So 25 Ankanams = 1800 Sq Ft, and 36 Ankanams = approximately 2592 Sq Ft.
